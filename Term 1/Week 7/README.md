**Ethical reflection - what are the risks of your tool? Who could it harm?**
The first risk is that the app invites people to get closer to insects. To take a good photo, someone might walk up to a hornet, a bee or a nest. They could get stung, which is dangerous for people with allergies, or they could disturb the very animals we want to protect. We don’t want anyone to harm or interfere with bees or any other species, so the app tells people to keep a few meters away, to take the photo from a distance, and never to touch or remove a nest themselves.
