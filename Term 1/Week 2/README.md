We focused on SDG 3, specifically 3.2. Which states; By 2030, end the epidemics of AIDS, tuberculosis, malaria and neglected tropical diseases and combat hepatitis, water-borne diseases and other communicable diseases.

**Ethical reflection - what are the risks of your tool? Who could it harm?**
_Every hackathon requires this. One honest paragraph beats three vague ones._

Within our system AI is being used first to simple forward information, based on information already written and decided by humans. After that it decides if that person needs more help based on the answers given by that person. Here it checks if the person is in distress or has any other issues or symptoms. If yes, then it suggests the patient to contact a professional. We feel like AI is not a doctor so it may only suggest contacting a doctor.
We would trust this system because it provides information based on a location and dates.
